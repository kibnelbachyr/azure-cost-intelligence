# Illustrative response contract

The following is a fictional illustration of the intended answer format, not a recorded model output or financial benchmark.

## Example: review cost opportunities for a single subscription

Actual spend for the selected period: EUR 1,000 (synthetic example; actual spend is not a savings estimate).

- **Review reservation eligibility** — explicit fictional monthly estimate EUR 25; low effort, low heuristic risk; advisory only. Verify commitment terms and utilization with the owner.
- **Review unattached disk** — monthly savings unknown; high risk if deletion is considered. Confirm ownership, dependencies, retention and recoverability; obtain explicit approval.
- **Advisor provider estimate** — amount 120 USD, period unknown; do not describe it as monthly or compare it numerically with EUR estimates.

No additive savings total or annualized total is supported. Missing price/period/currency is unknown, not zero. This example does not authorize any action. See [financial evidence contract](../docs/financial-evidence.md).
